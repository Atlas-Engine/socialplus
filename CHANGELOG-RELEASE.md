- The settings panel is reorganised into Display, PvP, Notifications and Scroll speed sections, each of which can be collapsed and stays that way. It is wider and much shorter, fits two settings to a row where they fit, and no longer leaves empty space below the last one when a section is hidden.
- Deleting a group now takes effect at once. Members used to cross out of it one at a time over the following minute while Battle.net saved each note, and one the server dropped could leave somebody in a group that no longer existed.
- Escape closes the settings panel again after the panel was first opened during combat.
