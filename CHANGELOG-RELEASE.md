- New: Snipe mode. A Display setting that puts In-game Friends directly under Favorites, above every group you made, so the characters you have added to watch are the first thing you see. A character friend needs nobody's acceptance, which is what makes that section the place to keep an eye on the names you are hoping to meet in the queue.
- VIP mode is two settings now, VIP crest and VIP title, so the crest over the portrait and the title in the title bar can be switched on separately. Anybody who had VIP mode off keeps both off.
- Character friends added this session now appear under Recently Added the way Battle.net friends do. They never did: the section they land in was being read as a group they had been filed into.
- Fixed typing in the search box forgetting every collapsed group. One letter was enough, and the groups came back expanded when the search was cleared.
- Fixed renaming a group dropping it to the bottom of the list. It keeps its place and its collapsed state, and the old name no longer lingers where an exact search for it showed nothing but headers.
- Fixed the Recently Added header being draggable, which wrote its internal name into the saved group order, and its cogwheel and right-click menu offering actions that could do nothing to it.
- Fixed the drop marker never appearing while dragging a group header, and the drag preview never showing its invite icon.
- Fixed turning SocialPlus notifications off switching Blizzard's own online and offline toasts back on at every login, for anybody who had them off.
- Fixed the friend right-click menu never being given its minimum width.
- Fixed the friends list occasionally failing to sort while Battle.net was still sending friend data, right after logging in.
- Invite All on a group header now picks the right character for a friend with several WoW accounts online, the same way the row's own invite button does.
- The Recently Added header and its clear button are translated on French and Spanish clients, and so are the VIP settings.
- Less work per keystroke while searching a large friends list, and less per redraw when no row is selected.
